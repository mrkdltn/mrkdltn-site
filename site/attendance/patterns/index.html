Sources and method

Sources. Department for Education, Pupil absence in schools in England, school-level files (); Schools, pupils and their characteristics and Special educational needs in England, school-level files for January 2026 (published June 2026). Open Government Licence v3.0.

Association, not cause. These charts show what goes with absence across schools. They do not show that any circumstance causes it, and they describe schools, not pupils: a pattern across schools need not hold for the children within them.

Groups by free school meals. Schools are grouped by their newest census figure and keep that group in every year, so the lines follow the same schools back in time. Where a school has exactly one predecessor its earlier years are included.

Similar schools. School pages set each primary and secondary beside its 20 nearest schools on the factors in the table above. The weights come from a regression of absence on those factors, used only to decide how much each factor should count; no expected rate is calculated for any school. Region is left out; ethnicity is never used. Special schools are not matched.

Small numbers. Schools with fewer than 10 pupils on roll are left out. Rates only.
