Method, in brief

What is counted. Overall absence is the share of school time missed, counted in half-days, for any reason. Persistent absence is the share of pupils who missed 10% or more of their half-days.

Which period. The figures at the top cover the autumn and spring terms of the latest school year, the newest the DfE has published for single schools. The chart shows full school years, which include the summer term, with the two-term figures marked beside them.

Similar schools. The 20 schools of the same kind most like this one in their share of pupils eligible for free school meals, with English as an additional language, with SEN support and with an education, health and care plan, and in size and setting. Region and ethnicity are not used. Their figure is for all 20 taken as one group.

Differences. A difference is worked out before rounding, so it can be 0.1 away from subtracting the two figures on the page. “Close to” means within 0.3 percentage points.

Small numbers. No figure is shown for a school with fewer than 10 pupils, and severe absence is never shown for a single school.

This is a reading of published figures, not a judgement on the school.
