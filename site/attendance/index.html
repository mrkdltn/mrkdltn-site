How this page works

What is counted. Overall absence is the share of possible sessions that pupils missed. A session is a morning or an afternoon. The figures cover pupils of compulsory school age in state-funded schools. “All” schools means primary, secondary and special schools together.

Authorised and unauthorised. Schools record each absence in the register as authorised or unauthorised, with a reason. Unauthorised absence here includes holidays the school did not agree, arriving after the register closed and absences with no reason given yet.

Points. A difference between two rates is given in percentage points, shortened to “points”. A rate of 7.0% against 6.5% is 0.5 points higher. Each difference is worked out from the rates before they are rounded and then rounded once, so it can be 0.1 away from what subtracting the two rates on screen would give.

This year so far, and last year. The figure for the year so far is the DfE’s own year-to-date total for each area. “The same point last year” is the total the DfE published at the same week of the school year 2025/26, so the two are like for like. The week-by-week and day-by-day charts are drawn from the daily figures; the DfE withholds a few days in some areas, so those charts can add up to slightly less than the totals. Term dates differ between years and between areas, so read small differences early in a term with caution.

Colours. Green marks lower absence than the comparison and rust higher. On the calendar and the map the comparison is England’s average for the year so far. On the chart of local authorities it is the same authority at the same point last year. Colour shows direction only.

Areas. England, its regions and the 153 local authorities, as the DfE publishes them. Counties, constituencies and wards are not in these in-year figures. Their pages use the termly statistics.

Source. Department for Education, Pupil attendance in schools, data set “Reasons for absence and attendance”, . These are official statistics drawn from the registers that state-funded schools share with the DfE each day. The DfE can revise them in later releases. Open Government Licence v3.0.

Map. The hexagon layout is adapted from Open Innovations (MIT licence) and updated to the 153 local authorities of 2026.

What is withheld. A day is left blank where an area had under 80% of its usual sessions, and a week where it had under 70%. When holidays are staggered, the pupils in school are not typical of the whole. Nothing is shown on fewer than 100 possible sessions. Rates are shown, never counts of pupils.

Not the termly statistics. These in-year figures are not the DfE’s termly absence statistics from the school census. The termly statistics are the accredited record and the only source for individual schools. School pages and the pages for trusts and for other areas use those.

Not a judgement. Every figure here is a reading of published data. None is a judgement on a school or an area.
