Sources and method

Source. Department for Education, Pupil attendance in schools, data set “Reasons for absence and attendance”, . Official statistics drawn from the registers that state-funded schools share with the DfE each day. Open Government Licence v3.0.

What is counted. Absence is the share of possible sessions (mornings and afternoons) that pupils missed. “All” schools is primary, secondary and special together. The figures cover pupils of compulsory school age and can be revised by the DfE in later releases.

Last year. “The same point last year” is the school year 2025/26 up to the same calendar date. Term dates differ between years and between areas, so small differences early in a term deserve caution.

What is withheld. A day is left blank where an area had under 80% of its usual sessions, and a week where it had under 70%, because the pupils in school when holidays are staggered are not typical of the whole. Nothing is shown on fewer than 100 possible sessions. Rates are shown, never counts of pupils.

Not the termly statistics. These in-year figures are not the same as the DfE’s termly absence statistics from the school census, which are the accredited record and the only source for individual schools. School pages and trust and constituency pages draw on those.
