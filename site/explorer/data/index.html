Rebuild any number

Every figure on a school page can be worked out from these files.

  • Gap to national: a8 minus the England average, which is the average of a8 across secondaries.csv weighted by pupils (47.6 for 2025/26). It is given as nat_diff.
  • Similar schools: take the school's 50 from similar-schools.csv, average their a8 (ss_mean) and subtract it from the school's (ss_diff). The reading uses the standard error √(14.2² ÷ pupils + ss_sd² ÷ 50): above or below beyond 1.96 standard errors, well above or well below when the gap is also larger than ss_sd. 14.2 is the pupil-level spread of Attainment 8, from the DfE's 2023/24 Progress 8 confidence intervals.
  • Contextual model: expected Attainment 8 is the intercept plus each factor times its coefficient in model.csv. English and maths 5+ uses the logistic form given there, so it stays between 0% and 100%. The result is in line within ±2 Attainment 8 points (±4 percentage points for English and maths) for a cohort of 180, scaled by √(180 ÷ pupils).
  • Likely ranges: each gap plus or minus the margin its reading uses: 1.96 standard errors for national and similar schools, the cohort-scaled band for the contextual model, and the DfE's published interval (p8lo_24 to p8hi_24) for Progress 8.

The code that builds the files is open (github.com/mrkdltn/mrkdltn-site, MIT licence). Check out the code version above and run python3 scripts/build_data.py, then python3 scripts/test_downloads.py, which rebuilds the similar-schools figures and both models from the CSVs and checks them.

What is not here

  • Groups under 10 pupils. The Attainment 8 of disadvantaged pupils (dis_a8), or of other pupils (nondis_a8), is left blank wherever that group is estimated at fewer than 10 pupils (the GCSE cohort times the disadvantaged share, rounded).
  • Pupil-level data. Everything here is published at school level. The official similar-schools groups use pupil-level data that is not public; these are an open replica.
  • POLAR4, one factor in the official similar-schools model, is not public and is left out; its weight is shared among the others.
  • Ethnicity is not used in matching or in any expected score.

Sources and dates

  • DfE, Key stage 4 performance 2025/26 (provisional, 28 October 2026), institution level, with 2024/25 and 2023/24 final. Provisional results are revised in February 2027, when the data will be rebuilt.
  • DfE, Schools, pupils and their characteristics, January 2026.
  • DfE, Special educational needs in England, January 2026.
  • Ofsted, management information, latest inspections as at 31 October 2026, with report cards published since then read from Ofsted's reports website each morning.
  • DfE, Get Information about Schools, daily extract, for local authority, region, trust, district, constituency and location.
  • DfE and Ofsted, Similar schools model for attainment: technical note (16 July 2026).

Each file's columns are traced to these sources in the dictionary. The build records a SHA-256 checksum for every input file in build.json.

Licence and how to cite

The downloads contain public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0. The Explorer's own derived figures (the similar-schools groups and averages, readings, the contextual models and their expected values) are released on the same terms, so you can copy, adapt and republish everything here, commercially or not, provided you acknowledge the source.

Please cite as: Ofsted Explorer data, mrkdltn.com/explorer/data/, data version [version]. Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0.

None of these figures is a judgement on a school, and none should be read as one. One year of provisional results is noisy; look for patterns over time.
