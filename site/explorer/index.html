    How to read this

    Raw results are not the school's effectAttainment 8 tracks intake closely. The national comparison mostly tells you about the intake.
    Similar schools ask a fairer questionHow did schools with a comparable intake do? It is a comparison, not a judgement, and Ofsted says it does not decide grades.
    One year is noisyA cohort of 150 pupils can move several points from year to year for reasons nobody controls. Look for patterns over years.
    This is a replicaThe official groups use pupil-level data that is not public. Treat these neighbours as a close approximation and check the IDSR.
    How the comparisons are built: method, sources and limits

    1. Similar schools: what each factor counts for

    The published weights from the DfE and Ofsted technical note. POLAR4 is not public, so the replica leaves it out and shares its weight among the rest in the same proportions.

    2. Similar schools: how this school’s 50 are chosen

    Similar schools, open replica. Following the DfE and Ofsted technical note (16 July 2026): each factor is standardised against the national mean and standard deviation; schools are matched by weighted squared Euclidean distance; each school's 50 nearest neighbours form its group, and the comparator is their average Attainment 8. Published weights: prior attainment 0.46, FSM6 0.14, POLAR4 0.11, SEN support 0.09, pupil stability 0.06, IDACI 0.06, EHC plans 0.04, EAL 0.03, pupil numbers 0.02. Public proxies used: prior attainment profile of the 2023/24 GCSE cohort (carried from a predecessor school where the URN changed; estimated for 34 schools); disadvantaged and non-mobile shares of the 2025/26 GCSE cohort; Ofsted's pupil-based IDACI quintile; January 2026 census pupil numbers and EAL; January 2026 SEN support and EHC plans. POLAR4 is left out and the other weights rescaled. A gap counts as significant when it exceeds 1.96 standard errors (pupil-level spread 14.2 points, estimated from 2023/24 Progress 8 confidence intervals, plus the group's spread); "well above" or "well below" also needs a gap larger than one standard deviation of the group.

    3. Contextual model: how this school’s expected result is built

    Each bar is one factor’s push on the expectation, largest first, starting from a school at the England average on every factor. It follows the results measure chosen above.

    Contextual model. One model of results on the same factors across all secondaries, in the spirit of the DfE's contextual value added measure of 2006 to 2010. For Attainment 8 it is a straight-line regression that explains about three quarters of the variation between schools; for a median-sized GCSE cohort a gap within 2 points reads as in line. For English and maths at grade 5 or above it is fitted to the share of pupils (a fractional logit), so expected values stay between 0% and 100%; for a median-sized cohort a gap within 4 points reads as in line. Both bands scale with the square root of cohort size, wider for smaller cohorts and narrower for larger ones, because a smaller cohort’s average moves more by chance.

    Progress 8. The last published figure, for 2023/24, carried from a predecessor school where the URN changed. The DfE did not publish it for 2024/25 or 2025/26.
