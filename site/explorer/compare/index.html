Two or three schools, side by side

Each school’s report card, results and intake, with every result read against that school’s own comparators: England, its 50 similar schools and a contextual model.

Schools serve different intakes, so the fairest comparison is how far each sits from schools like it, not the raw gap between them. Where a likely range includes zero, the gap is within what chance alone could give.

    Loading every secondary school…

    Likely ranges are 95%: against England, chance variation in one year’s pupils; against similar schools, that plus the spread of the 50; against the contextual model, the in-line band for a year group of that size. Report card grades are Ofsted’s, as published, in Ofsted’s colours. Secondary schools with 2025/26 GCSE results only. This is a reading of the data, not a judgement on any school.
