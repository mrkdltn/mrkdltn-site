How this site is made

This is a static site with no cookies and no tracking beyond privacy-respecting page counts. It is set in Libre Caslon Display, Source Serif 4 and Hanken Grotesk, all open typefaces served from this site.

Charts use Ofsted's own report-card colours, so that grades read here as they do on a report card. Every figure links to its primary source, and the data and method behind each piece are published with it.

The portrait is a photograph. Other imagery on this site is illustration, made and edited digitally; it does not depict a real school, place or person.

If you find an error, tell me and I will correct it.
