# For Mark to check: 30 labelled results sentences

These are 30 of the 150 results sentences labelled by hand to validate the frozen results and direction lexicons (`results-lexicon-v1.md`, analysis 7), drawn at random with seed 20261128. Each was labelled from the sentence alone, under the second part of `labelling-rules.md`, without its school, grades, phase, month or results in view, and before the lexicon was run on it. The text is as parsed, with names already removed and grade words shown as "[grade]" (so "the [grade]" in a key stage 2 sentence is the expected standard).

Two labels for each: whether the sentence refers to published results, and its direction about them (positive, negative, level, mixed, or none). Please mark whether you agree; where you disagree, your label is enough. The agreement rate goes into the results.
